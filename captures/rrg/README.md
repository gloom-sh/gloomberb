# Relative rotation captures

Real Cloud responses collected September 22, 2026: SPY and eleven sector ETFs,
USD listings, daily histories through September 21 and weekly metrics through
September 18. The isolated capture service replays those responses without
altering values. No synthetic prices or invented market states.

- rrg-board-terminal.png: tmux full pane
- rrg-detail-terminal.png: dated strength and momentum history
- rrg-board-kitty.png: native Kitty chart renderer

The capture process exercised selection, detail/back, refresh and narrow resize;
no React hook/update-depth or listener warnings. Capture processes are stopped.
