# Capture publication review

The frozen selection contains **4,318 source capture files representing 2,041 unique image hashes**. Every selected image is covered by OCR review, an exact-image manual decision, or a separately recorded full-frame review. The approval validates the current files against the frozen inventory and final comparison.

The final incremental OCR review covered 457 new image hashes and 715 source files, with no OCR failures. Ten flagged images were inspected in full; the matches were public contract, clinical-trial and power-project identifiers. **No selected image remains unresolved.**

Demo chat and news-story frames received full-frame inspection. Publication masks are bound to exact source-image hashes and reviewed previews. Raw OCR output, replay data and local proof paths remain outside the public bundle. Functional coverage and missing variants are recorded in the benchmark report.
