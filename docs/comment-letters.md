# SEC comment letters (CLTR)

`CLTR` lists the comment letters the SEC publishes on EDGAR: letters SEC staff send a company about its filings (form `UPLOAD`) and the company's correspondence back (form `CORRESP`), which besides answers to comments includes requests such as accelerating a registration statement. The SEC releases a review's letters no sooner than 20 business days after the review is complete, so the newest letters are weeks old and a review still under way is not listed. The From column says who wrote each letter.

With nothing typed, the pane lists the newest letters from every company. Words in the search box are matched against the letters' full text by EDGAR full-text search, best match first; put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly. A ticker on its own (`CLTR AAPL`) lists that company's letters, newest first, as EDGAR files them under the ticker; to search the text for a word that is also a ticker, put it in quotes. More letters load as you scroll. EDGAR full-text search pages through at most 10,000 matches, and the pane warns when a search reaches that limit.

Enter opens a letter and shows its text with the documents' own wording. Staff letters are usually PDFs, which the pane cannot show; `o` opens any letter on sec.gov. Mentions lists accounting and disclosure topics whose words appear in the letter's text (restatement, going concern, revenue recognition, non-GAAP, impairment and similar). It is a word match, not an SEC classification: it says the letter uses the term, not that the staff found a problem, how serious it is, or how the review ended. A letter whose text could not be read has no Mentions line.
